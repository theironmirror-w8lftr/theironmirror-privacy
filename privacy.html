The Iron Mirror Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

The Iron Mirror does not collect, store, or share any of your personal information. This policy explains how the app handles your information and applies to The Iron Mirror app for Android and The Iron Mirror website.

Information we collect

We do not collect any information. The workouts, sets, weights, body measurements, custom programs and settings you enter are saved only on your own device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see them.

Accounts, ads, and tracking

  • The app does not require an account or login.
  • The app does not show ads.
  • The app does not use analytics, tracking, or third-party data collection tools.
  • The app does not connect to the internet on its own. It only goes online when you tap one of the links described below.

The app is downloaded through Google Play. Google may collect information under its own privacy policy, which we do not control.

Videos and links you choose to open

Some exercises have a button or link to a demonstration video on YouTube, and you can add your own YouTube video to an exercise. When you open or add a video, your device connects to YouTube, which may receive standard technical information such as your IP address under the Google Privacy Policy. Nothing about your workouts is sent.

Copying, sharing, and backing up your workouts

These only happen when you choose to do them:

  • Copy for AI copies a saved workout so you can paste it into another app.
  • Open in Claude opens the Claude website (claude.ai) with that workout and any notes you typed already filled in.
  • Download Backup and other exports save a file wherever you pick, such as your phone's Downloads folder or Google Drive.

Once your workout leaves the app this way, the privacy policy of that app, website, or storage service applies. We never receive it.

Deleting your data

Because your information is stored only on your device, you control it. Uninstalling the app, clearing its data in your phone's settings, or using Clear All Data in the app deletes everything the app has saved, and we cannot recover it. Backup files you saved outside the app are not deleted; remove those yourself if you want them gone.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the updated version on this page and change the effective date at the top.

Contact
